Meatloaf Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix the ground beef, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, onion, and garlic.
  3. Season with salt and black pepper.
  4. Transfer the mixture to a greased loaf pan.
  5. Bake for 1 hour or until the internal temperature reaches 160°F (70°C).
